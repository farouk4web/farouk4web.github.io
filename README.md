# Farouk Abdelhamid — Portfolio

This is the personal portfolio website of **Farouk Abdelhamid**, a Full-Stack Developer based in Cairo, Egypt.

## What it is

The site is a single-page showcase of Farouk's professional identity and career. It presents visitors with a clear picture of who he is as a developer and what he has accomplished over more than four years in the industry.

The portfolio is organized into clear sections:

- **Hero** — a quick introduction with his name, title, and a short tagline, plus quick links to his resume and social profiles.
- **About** — a personal bio explaining his background, experience, and work ethic.
- **Stats** — key numbers that summarize his career and reach at a glance.
- **Skills** — the areas he works in, grouped by category.
- **Experience** — his professional journey, role by role.
- **Projects** — a collection of the work he has built, each with a summary and a link to try it or view the code.
- **Contact** — how to get in touch with him directly.

It also pulls in his latest work from GitHub so the showcased projects stay up to date.

## Who it's for

The site is aimed at anyone interested in Farouk as a professional:

- **Hiring managers and recruiters** looking to understand his background, experience, and capabilities at a glance.
- **Clients and companies** evaluating him for full-stack or backend development work, both freelance and full-time.
- **Fellow developers and collaborators** curious about his projects and the kinds of systems he builds.
- **Anyone** wanting a direct, easy way to reach out to him.

In short, it serves as Farouk's professional front door — a place where people can learn about his experience, see examples of his work, and contact him for opportunities.
